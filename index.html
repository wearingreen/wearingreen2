Why Salt Lake Brewers Club was created

I wanted a way for curious individuals to try my coffee that I have been refining over the years without it being transactional. I am relatively new to the city of Salt Lake City, having recently resided in Austin; I wanted to put myself out there to make friends with similar interests. So because of that, I have embarked on a journey to search for like-minded coffee nerds around the area to learn from, share, and collaborate for mutual benefit: We get to meet people who are into the same craft, and we get to try coffees we otherwise would not have tried ourselves by trying each other's personal brewing preferences and tastes. This motive led me to create a space to bring it all together and build a consistent routine to network, make new friends, try new coffees, and teach newcomers and pros.

~ Drake Marshall
