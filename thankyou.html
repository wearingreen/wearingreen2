Workshops These are the days we teach members concepts related to brewing coffee in hopes to expand our members understanding of coffee. Small Group Tastings This is our early attempt to mimic a cafe private session experience. You with a small group of members can try elegant coffees featured on the menu from the owner. All drinks will be free with just the requirement of a 10 dollar donation. Varies, there will be days on the calender that will highlight when tastings are avaiable $10
